## What the source actually proposes

For every one of the 256 unchanged candidates:

1. Compute active superpoints from the existing fused Text/Query Mask probability > 0.5 and reference-valid flag. For each of the six axis-aligned faces, identify every active SP whose **actual input-member lower/upper coordinate** equals the relevant minimum/maximum. Retain ties. Pool projected SP content, Mask responses/disagreement, member counts, and directional positions using each source SP's full member count. These are **source-SP features**, not newly extracted features of the individual extremal points.
2. Encode six source tokens. A shared 105→64→32→1 MLP reads two opposing-face tokens, the candidate query, two-box geometry, source fractions, reference validity, and axis identity. Clamp its three outputs into [0,1]. For each axis k, use the same coefficient a_k for center and size:
   `c_k=(1-a_k)c_mask,k+a_k c_native,k`,
   `d_k=(1-a_k)d_mask,k+a_k d_native,k`.
   Consequently both endpoints on an axis also share a_k. This is a **three-degree-of-freedom convex geometry family**, not six independent face distributions.
3. The separate selected-query Mask objective adds the existing own/fused focal–Dice terms only when the native bbs winner is unmatched to **every** original GT assignment. A winner matched to root or another instance keeps its original role. This introduces one extra root Mask responsibility at most; it does not introduce a new score or ranking head.

The mixer has 29,793 parameters and 14 state tensors. Its parent is frozen; Mask logits, query content inputs, geometry, and original semantic scores are not trained through this new box head. The box objective reuses actual frozen-parent matching and (10 L1 + 2 GIoU)/7 on originally matched GT instances. It does **not** add selected-unmatched root geometry supervision. C3 and the mixer are therefore currently connected through the parent Mask/support state, not through demonstrated joint optimization or an established interaction.

The zero output layer initializes a=0 and preserves the Mask box. The proposed two-step real-batch gradient/restore check has not run in this review. Runtime viability must still be checked; source arithmetic is not an M0 PASS.

## Nearest primary mechanisms and distinctions

**EG-3DVG (CVPR 2026) is the closest whole-method geometry neighbor.** Section 3.3 selects the text-aligned candidate, maps SP mask values to member points, forms an enclosing mask box, and mixes it with an MLP-regressed box using a fixed 0.5 coefficient in Eq. (3). Thus “Mask-to-box,” using member extents, and regression/Mask fusion are already present in the same 3D grounding setting. The proposed additional content is provenance-dependent *axis* weighting, not first box/Mask cooperation. Its expression/geometry attention and contrastive modules are different from this small frozen-parent head. [Primary paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Park_EG-3DVG_Expression_and_Geometry_Aware_Grounding_Decoder_for_3D_Visual_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf)

**BorderDet (ECCV 2020), SABL (ECCV 2020), and Not Every Side Is Equal (ICCV 2023) constrain C1's wording.** BorderAlign samples a predicted 2D box border and takes channelwise feature maxima; these are not indices obtained by minimizing/maximizing the observed Mask members' coordinates. SABL aggregates RoI features along each axis, splits them into side features, and predicts a bucket and offset for each side. Not Every Side Is Equal is an especially relevant 3D neighbor: its SoI pooling interpolates nearby seed features to generated side points, combines geometry with side-distribution statistics, and learns side uncertainty for pseudo-label loss weights. The current code selects exact SP provenance and predicts three box-mixture coefficients; it has neither that six-side distribution nor supervised uncertainty targets. [BorderDet §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.11056v1), [SABL §3](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2020/papers_ECCV/papers/123490392.pdf), [Not Every Side Is Equal §3.2–3.3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/papers/Wang_Not_Every_Side_Is_Equal_Localization_Uncertainty_Estimation_for_Semi-Supervised_ICCV_2023_paper.pdf)

**Generic weighted localization is not new.** Weighted boxes fusion averages clustered boxes using detection confidence. He et al.'s CVPR 2019 *Bounding Box Regression with Uncertainty for Accurate Object Detection* performs coordinate-specific variance voting among neighboring boxes. UA3D (ICCV 2025) estimates seven-coordinate disagreement between primary and auxiliary detectors and uses it to reweight noisy pseudo-box training losses. The present coefficients interpolate two already corresponding predictions of one query directly at inference. They are not calibrated uncertainty, Gaussian variance, or an auxiliary-detector disagreement model. “Trust” should mean a learned interpolation coefficient only. [WBF §3](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.13302v3), [variance voting §3](https://arxiv.org/html/1809.08545v2), [UA3D official §2.1–2.2](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Zhang_Harnessing_Uncertainty-aware_Bounding_Boxes_for_Unsupervised_3D_Object_Detection_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf)

**ExtremeNet, H3DNet, Spherical Mask, and RefineMask do not contain the reviewed mixture, but defeat broad first-boundary claims.** ExtremeNet predicts extreme/center keypoints and groups them geometrically, including edge aggregation for nonunique extrema. H3DNet predicts center/face/edge primitives and fits/refines proposals. Spherical Mask predicts radial extent sectors and virtually migrates points to repair coarse masks; its target ray lengths use furthest foreground members. RefineMask progressively fuses fine semantic/instance features and focuses refinement on mask boundaries. These differ from inspecting the existing selected Mask's extremal SP owners and interpolating its box with its own regression output. [ExtremeNet §4](https://arxiv.org/html/1901.08043v1), [H3DNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.05682v2), [Spherical Mask §3.3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Shin_Spherical_Mask_Coarse-to-Fine_3D_Point_Cloud_Instance_Segmentation_with_Spherical_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf), [RefineMask §3.2–3.3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/papers/Zhang_RefineMask_Towards_High-Quality_Instance_Segmentation_With_Fine-Grained_Features_CVPR_2021_paper.pdf)

**PV-Ground/MCLN, UniVLG, Mask DINO, D-FINE, and 3DRefTR are inherited/contextual work.** PV-Ground explicitly adopts MCLN's multi-task head/loss; the official code already produces separate regression and Text/Query Mask paths. MCLN's adaptive mask fusion is not new here. UniVLG already describes distant unrelated Mask points and multi-instance contamination, and applies enclosing-box L1/GIoU supervision. Mask DINO derives decoder reference boxes from masks; D-FINE predicts and refines edge distributions; 3DRefTR predicts query/SP masks and boxes selected by referring scores. These prevent claims of first mask/box collaboration, first support-conditioned query prediction, first outlier-induced extent errors, or first distribution refinement. [PV-Ground §3.4](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Shang_PV-Ground_Text-Guided_Point-Voxel_Interaction_for_3D_Visual_Grounding_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf), [official model source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AaNnWwTt/PV-Ground/main/models/pv_ground.py), [MCLN](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.05363v1), [UniVLG §3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.10745v1), [Mask DINO §3.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.02777v3), [D-FINE §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13842v1), [3DRefTR](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.11887v2)

**C3 is narrower than one-to-many or inference-aware training.** MS-DETR chooses extra positives using class/IoU matching and top-K thresholding, rather than the unchanged deployed winner. iFAN learns mask quality and ranks matched predictions above hard unmatched competitors; its ranking gradients are restricted to the quality branch. The reviewed C3 instead repairs the chosen unmatched mask while preserving scores. 3DRP-Net uses IoU-ranked soft labels for referring-score learning. These are real neighbors, but none of these read mechanisms is the exact one-winner/unmatched-only/root-Mask-only rule. The existing matched-only versus C3 training can test effectiveness, but alone cannot show that *winner selection* is better than an equal extra-positive budget. [MS-DETR §3.2–3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.03989v1), [iFAN method](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03216v1), [3DRP-Net §3.3–3.4](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.656.pdf)

## Direct controls that would make the thin delta carry

The proposed first pilot is appropriately small. After the active experiment closes and source/runtime gates pass, compare **whole_support versus extremal_support**, with one identical frozen-parent forward per batch, the same original assignment, exact same 14-state initialization, identical 29,793-parameter head, fit rows, optimizer budget, and seed2027. Keep all 256 candidates and native scores. Report a single final box rule per model; do not select an output condition using validation GT.

At the formal endpoint, save same-forward outputs for native regression, Mask, fixed 0.5, whole-support adaptive mixing, and extremal-source adaptive mixing. Record both thresholds, per-row repairs/damages, reference validity, source ties/counts, raw/clamped coefficients, saturation rates, unchanged native winner, and Mask equality. This establishes whether specific evidence improves geometry rather than merely adding capacity.

If that pilot is positive, the next direct controls should answer the unresolved claims rather than expand architecture:

- **C1 provenance specificity:** compare against the previous face/center-neighborhood readout or a fixed-budget generic boundary/SP readout under the same head. A matched-source-mass shuffle/random-source control can distinguish true face-owner information from sparse pooling alone. Use one clear control, not an uncontrolled menu.
- **C2 adaptive-axis necessity:** use train-only learned global/scalar mixing and a geometry/query-only gate. Tie three coefficients to one when testing axis dependence. The whole-support comparison alone does not establish that three-axis mixing is better than a simpler adaptive blend. Do not choose constants using the validation diagnostic.
- **C3 selection specificity:** retain the matched-only same-budget continuation, and if claiming a separate winner-responsibility contribution, compare with an equally sized extra-unmatched supervision budget selected by a prespecified alternative. Track root-matched, other-matched, and completely-unmatched winners separately; direct output-gradient locality does not guarantee no shared-parameter effects on other candidates.
- **Combination versus addition:** only claim C3×provenance synergy after a controlled C3 off/on × whole/extremal comparison from matched parent states. Running the mixer after C3 is sequential composition, not interaction evidence.
- **Mechanistic finding:** use saved prediction-time provenance to test whether actual current false-positive extremal sources, truncation, or branch disagreement explain coefficient changes and paired repairs. Any GT source labeling is analysis-only; evaluate full validation as well as predefined groups.

These are conditional evidentiary requirements, not authorization to launch more jobs or a demand to complete every ablation before an early pilot. Keep the current source minimal. Real two-step gradients, preserved parent outputs, integrated call signature, source-mode/parent identity on restore, and a completed endpoint remain separate engineering/experimental gates.

## Positioning and final scope

A reviewer-verifiable positioning sentence is:

**“For a fixed language-conditioned 3D candidate, we test whether the observed superpoints that generate its predicted Mask extrema provide useful evidence for axis-wise interpolation between its native regression and Mask extent, while separately repairing Mask supervision for an otherwise unmatched deployed winner.”**

A successful result could establish a useful combination and finding about geometry errors under fixed candidate selection. At present C1/C2 are intertwined parts of one untrained head, C3 has no supplied endpoint, and their interaction is untested. Counting code files, six tokens, three axes, or one training rule does not establish three independently effective contributions. The full target and later Nr3D/Sr3D training are outside this novelty PASS and remain incomplete.
